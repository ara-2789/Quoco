3. DID THE RESEND TAP'S onSubmit RUN BEFORE THE REDIRECT? Not decidable by reading. If the resend button was disabled (latch held, pending-controls.tsx:110-115), no submit event fires at all. By READ the observed sequence should therefore be impossible; so either the latch was NOT held / the button was NOT disabled at the moment of the resend tap, or the tap landed after the remount.

POST 2 showing ...&error=WRONG_CODE means it executed after verify's redirect committed. That fits an action that was CALLED at tap time and queued behind verify (Next runs server actions one after another: believed, not verified here) as well as a tap made after the remount; the rows cannot tell the two apart.
